# Adversarial and Real-User Assurance Baseline

This is a mandatory **applicability and traceability baseline** for every QA Master Checklist invocation. It does not authorize execution. In discovery mode, classify and trace the surfaces; in plan mode, design cases; in release mode, inventory independent execution evidence and owners.

Use stable IDs `ARU-01` through `ARU-19`. For every ID record one status:

- `APPLICABLE_TRACED`: evidenced surface, risk, owner and planned/existing case are linked.
- `APPLICABLE_GAP`: evidenced or materially plausible surface lacks a trace, approved oracle, safe environment or case.
- `UNKNOWN`: source/environment/product evidence is insufficient; create an owned business or engineering question.
- `OUT_OF_SCOPE_WITH_OWNER`: excluded by the authorized scope, with owner and rationale; do not imply product-wide coverage.
- `NOT_APPLICABLE_WITH_EVIDENCE`: the product genuinely lacks the surface, citing the inspected inventory.

Do not use one passing happy-path test to cover an assurance family. Coverage must identify the actor, tenant/resource, state, channel, trust boundary, observable user result, persisted/side-effect result, recovery behavior and evidence source. Keep planning, existing automated coverage, authorized execution, independent assessment and human acceptance as separate evidence layers.

| ARU-14 | Public and indirect resource access | Inventory intentionally unauthenticated or tokenized QR, share, download, media, document and webhook endpoints. | ARU-16 | Production-sized load, stress, soak and recovery | Derive an approved workload/data/concurrency model, including hot tenants and period-end bursts. Never invent targets or run against production by default. | Performance/reliability plan; SRE + Product. |
| ARU-17 | Hosted-provider configuration and control-plane security | For actual Render/Vercel/Neon/Upstash/Cloudflare or equivalent services, inspect environment isolation, IAM/token scope, secret handling, TLS, public exposure, cache/queue/storage settings, preview deployments, logs, backups and rollback. Automated browser tests do not count as human UAT. | UAT plan/evidence/signoff; Product owner + named business representatives. |

## Cross-family interactions

Do not treat the families as independent when risk comes from their intersection. Mandate known hazardous combinations, such as revoked session + cross-tenant object ID, stale tab + concurrent approval, hostile filename + public download, formula injection + tenant export, offline replay + role deactivation, large export + rate-limit fail-open, or hosted preview deployment + production secrets. Pairwise sampling may reduce benign combinations but must not remove known high-harm intersections.

## Mode expectations

- `discover`: produce the 19-row applicability register, source/entrypoint inventory, top gaps and owner questions; do not invent final cases or claim execution.
- `plan`: trace every applicable P0/P1 family to risks, rules/decisions, architecture failure modes and executable `Not Tested` cases; document safe environments and authorization gates.
- `incremental`: reassess the families touched directly or indirectly by the diff, plus any changed auth, tenant, file, export, offline, client or deployment boundary.
- `release`: require execution provenance for applicable release gates; independent penetration and human UAT remain separate evidence types and cannot be replaced by automation.
